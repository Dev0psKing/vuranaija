## Slide 2: The Problem
**Headline:** The "Saving is Losing" Trap
**Key Insight:** In a high-inflation economy, saving money in a bank is a guaranteed loss.
*   **The Reality:** Inflation in Nigeria is consistently double-digit (e.g., ~20-30%).

*   **The Barrier:** Most youth view "Investing" as either too complex, too risky (crypto scams), or "only for the rich."
